## Unified Cross-Chain Infrastructure for DeFi

Warp is building the infrastructure layer that makes multi-chain crypto feel like a single, seamless network. Our mission is to reduce the fragmentation, complexity, and privacy gaps that affect today's blockchain ecosystems.

Instead of forcing users to navigate dozens of chains, swap between incompatible tokens, or sacrifice privacy for convenience, Warp aims to provide a unified experience built on solver-based liquidity networks, deterministic settlement contracts, and zero-knowledge technology.

Whether you're a developer building cross-chain applications, a merchant accepting crypto payments, or a user transferring assets between networks, Warp is designed to deliver speed, simplicity, and progressively stronger privacy at every layer.

<Note>
  Warp is under active development. Features marked **Coming Soon**, **Planned**, or **Roadmap** are not yet available, and their descriptions reflect design goals rather than current behavior.

## Why Warp?

### Universal Access

One integration is designed to unlock support for many blockchains. Users and developers should not need to worry about chain selection, token compatibility, or manual bridging.

### Fast Settlement

Solvers fulfill transfers from their own liquidity on the destination chain, so users are not left waiting on slow cross-chain messaging. On the roadmap, zero-knowledge proofs are intended to enable near-real-time payment confirmation.

### Privacy by Design _(Roadmap)_

As Warp evolves, transfers, payments, and proofs are planned to flow through privacy-preserving routing layers, designed to make transactions harder to link on-chain and keep their details confidential.

### Built for Developers

Clean REST APIs and smart contract primitives designed to abstract complexity while preserving flexibility and control.

## Our Vision

A private, interoperable, cross-chain layer that feels as seamless as using a single blockchain, without users ever needing to notice the complexity underneath. Use Warp's components individually, or combine them to build secure cross-chain experiences.
