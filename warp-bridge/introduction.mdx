## Warp Bridge

**Move Crypto Across Chains — Without KYC, Complexity, or Pooled Liquidity**

Warp Bridge is a cross-chain transfer protocol designed for speed, simplicity, and progressive privacy. Instead of depending on the large, idle liquidity pools that traditional bridges rely on, Warp Bridge uses a **lock-and-fulfill** model: the user's tokens are locked in a contract on the source chain, a permissionless solver delivers the destination tokens to the recipient from its own liquidity, and the locked funds are released to the solver once the fulfillment has been verified. See [Protocol](/warp-bridge/protocol) for the full design.

<Note>
  Warp Bridge is under active development. The Bridge API, widget, and contract integrations are **Coming Soon**, and features marked **Planned** or **Roadmap** are not yet available.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Our mission is to make cross-chain movement as easy as a swap, while gradually rolling out privacy layers designed to make transfers harder to link as network activity grows.
</Tip>

## The Cross-Chain Experience

Warp Bridge replaces the traditional "lock-and-wait" bridging model with a solver-driven flow designed for both everyday users and developers.

## Advantages

- **Fast Settlement** — Solvers deliver destination funds from their own liquidity, so users are not left waiting on long cross-chain confirmation windows.
- **Seamless, No-KYC UX** — Just connect, swap, and go. No accounts. No sign-ups.
- **Low Slippage and Fair Pricing** — Solvers compete to fill orders, which is designed to deliver competitive rates and minimize volatility impact.
- **Scales With Demand** — The architecture is designed to grow with network activity as more solvers participate.
- **Native Token Transfers** — Avoid unnecessary wrapped or custodial assets wherever possible.
- **Reduced Risk Exposure** — Funds are locked per order rather than pooled, avoiding the large shared TVL that makes traditional bridges attractive targets.
- **Privacy that Gets Stronger Over Time** _(Roadmap)_ — As more users transact, transfers are designed to blend together, making them harder to link.
- **Order + Call Execution** _(Planned)_ — Let a transfer trigger actions automatically on the destination chain — e.g., deposit into a vault, stake, execute a trade, or interact with a contract.

Warp Bridge aims to provide a fast, simple, and progressively private cross-chain experience.

## For Developers

Warp Bridge is designed to offer a lightweight, developer-friendly API _(Coming Soon)_ that removes the complexity of cross-chain interactions. It is planned to let you:

- Request cross-chain quotes in seconds
- Execute transfers with minimal latency
- Track every step of a transfer's lifecycle
- Integrate privacy-enhanced routing as new layers roll out _(Roadmap)_
- Build cross-chain apps without touching low-level RPCs or complex contract calls

Warp Bridge handles the infrastructure. You focus on your product.
