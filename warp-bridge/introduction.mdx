Warp Bridge is a planned cross-chain transfer protocol. Instead of a large shared liquidity pool, it would use a **lock-and-fulfill** model: the user's tokens are locked in a contract on the source chain, a permissionless solver delivers the destination tokens to the recipient from its own liquidity, and the locked tokens are released to the solver once the fulfillment is verified.

## Design goals

- **Fast delivery** — the recipient would be paid from solver liquidity as soon as the fulfillment transaction confirms, without waiting for cross-chain messaging.
- **No KYC** — users would connect a wallet and transfer, with no account.
- **Competitive pricing** — solvers would compete to fill orders.
- **Per-order risk** — funds would be locked per order rather than pooled, avoiding a large shared balance that attracts attacks.
- **Native tokens** — transfers would avoid wrapped assets where possible.
- **Order + call execution** — a transfer could trigger an action on the destination chain, such as a deposit or a stake.
- **Privacy** — later versions may add routing that makes transfers harder to link on-chain.

## Planned developer access

Warp Bridge is planned to offer a REST API for quotes, transfer submission and status tracking, an embeddable widget, and direct contract integration. None of these are available yet. See [Integration options](/warp-bridge/usecases).
