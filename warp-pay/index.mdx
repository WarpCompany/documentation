## Pay With Warp

Pay With Warp is a checkout solution that lets customers pay using a supported token on a supported chain, while merchants receive their preferred asset. You create a payment session with the [Checkout API](/api-reference/checkout-api/create-payment-session), send the customer to Warp's hosted checkout, and track the payment until it completes. Over time, Pay With Warp is designed to use Warp Bridge for cross-chain routing and conversion, with crypto-to-fiat payouts planned.

<Note>
  Warp is under active development. Features marked **Coming Soon** or **Roadmap** are not yet available, and their descriptions reflect design goals rather than current behavior.
</Note>

Pay With Warp is designed around three long-term priorities:

### 1. Universal Access

Warp removes the burden of choosing chains, swapping tokens, or bridging manually. One integration is designed to unlock multi-chain support for users and developers alike.

### 2. Fast Settlement

Pay With Warp is designed for real-time payment experiences. On the roadmap, Warp plans to integrate zero-knowledge proofs to enable near-real-time payment confirmation, so merchants can release digital goods without waiting for full block finality.

### 3. Privacy by Design _(Roadmap)_

As Warp Bridge evolves, transactions — including Pay With Warp payments — are planned to flow through Warp's privacy layer, designed to make transfers harder to link on-chain and keep payment details confidential.

Under the hood, Warp Bridge uses a solver-based architecture to find efficient settlement routes, competitive pricing, and efficient use of liquidity. For developers and end users, this complexity is abstracted away behind a single interface.

To start accepting payments, follow the [Quickstart](/quickstart) and see the [Integration Examples](/warp-pay/examples).
