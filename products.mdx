Warp's products can be used independently. This page lists each one with its current status so you know what you can build on today.

| Product | Status | What you can do today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pay with Warp | <Badge color="green">Live</Badge> | Accept crypto payments through hosted checkout and the Warp API |
| ZK Proofs | <Badge color="blue">Beta</Badge> | Write, compile and deploy Circom/Groth16 verifiers; verify proofs on-chain or through the API |
| Warp Verify | <Badge color="blue">Beta</Badge> | Issue zero-knowledge attestations and check them publicly |

## Pay with Warp

<Badge color="green">Live</Badge>

Hosted crypto checkout for merchants. Your server creates a checkout session with a secret key, the customer pays on Warp's checkout page with MetaMask or any WalletConnect-compatible wallet, and Warp verifies the transaction on-chain before marking the session `completed`.

- **EVM chains** — Ethereum, Polygon, Base, Arbitrum and Optimism on mainnet, plus their testnets. See [Supported networks and tokens](/warp-pay#supported-networks-and-tokens).
- **Native tokens and stablecoins** — ETH (POL on Polygon), USDC, and USDT and DAI where deployed.
- **No conversion** — you receive the token the customer paid with, at the receiving address configured for your project.
- **Webhooks** — signed `payment.completed`, `payment.failed` and `payment.expired` events with automatic retries. See [Webhooks](/warp-pay/webhooks).

## ZK Proofs

<Badge color="blue">Beta</Badge>

Build zero-knowledge circuits in the Warp Platform and verify the resulting proofs.

- **Circom editor** — write circuits by hand or with the built-in AI assistant, then compile them in the browser.
- **Groth16 verifier deployment** — run the trusted setup and deploy a Solidity verifier to Sepolia or Base Sepolia (or a local Anvil chain during development).
- **Two ways to verify** — call `verifyProof` on your verifier contract directly, or use the hosted verification API, which returns a signed receipt.

## Warp Verify

<Badge color="blue">Beta</Badge>

Zero-knowledge attestations about real-world assets — ownership, compliance and provenance — that anyone can check without seeing the underlying data.

## On the roadmap

<Badge color="gray">Roadmap</Badge>

These are design proposals. They have no public implementation yet, and their design may change.

- **Warp Bridge** — a planned solver-based cross-chain transfer protocol. See [Warp Bridge](/warp-bridge/introduction).
- **ZK Validator Network** — a planned decentralized network for verifying zero-knowledge proofs. See [ZK Validator Network](/zkvalidator/introduction).
